## Visual Theme
Industrial hazard telemetry. High contrast, technical, authoritative dark-mode layout designed to evoke the precision of a professional crash reconstruction lab.

## Art Direction
- Concept: Tactical forensic evidence monitoring for high-stakes trucking litigation.
- Signature move: Telemetry Data Terminal Exhibit.
- Layout rhythm: Dense, structured, highly technical grid interfaces.

## Typography
- Scale: Editorial / Industrial
- h1: clamp(2.5rem, 5vw + 1rem, 4.5rem)
- h2: clamp(1.8rem, 3.5vw + 1rem, 3rem)
- h3: clamp(1.3rem, 2vw + 1rem, 2rem)
- Headlines: "Bricolage Grotesque" — Ultra-bold, aggressive sans-serif with raw industrial presence, matching the high-stakes litigation theme.
- Body: "JetBrains Mono" (for data/telemetry components) and "Inter" (for high-readability narrative copy).

## Components
- Buttons: Sharp, hard-angled square edges (0px radius). Heavy border outlines with contrasting solid block states on hover.
- Cards: Thin technical wire borders (`border-border`), dark charcoal surface offsets (`bg-surface`), and absolute-positioned tiny technical text labeling in the corners.
- Sections: Spacious padding `py-20 md:py-28 lg:py-36` to give massive editorial weight to high-density layouts.
- Imagery: High-contrast, desaturated photography with warm amber accents. Raw close-ups of commercial vehicle tires, cargo straps, warning signs, and damaged steel.

## Do's and Don'ts
- DO use high-visibility GGL red for accenting critical telemetry data points and CTAs.
- DO structure layouts as numbered exhibits and formal technical stages.
- DON'T use soft rounded corners, pastel colors, or gentle nature stock photography.
- DON'T use generic personal injury scales of justice or courtroom gavels.
